Johnathan "John" Miles is a man defined by a meticulous and aspirational "love" for James Bond, a passion that borders on lifestyle emulation. By day, John is a 44-year-old risk analyst in Chicago, a profession steeped in data and predictability, which he actively contrasts with the sophisticated, crisis-driven world of 007. This fixation is not just casual fandom; it is a dedicated curation of his personal aesthetic and environment. He invests significantly in the specific trappings of the character, favoring navy blue, single-breasted suits, brown suede chukka boots, and a specific Omega Seamaster Diver 300M, which he acquired after his last bonus. His evenings are often spent perfecting the Vesper martini—having researched the exact non-mainstream ingredients like Lillet Blanc—or streaming the John Barry instrumental scores. For John, the "love" for Bond is an appreciation for an ideal of precision, classic masculinity, and control, a framework he uses to elevate the everyday world around him.
